Frequently asked questions

How long does migration take?

Most studios complete their migration in 3-4 weeks. Our team handles everything including member data, payment information, and schedule transfer. Zero disruption to your business.

Will my members' payment data transfer?

Yes! All credit card data migrates seamlessly through our Stripe integration. Your members won't need to re-enter payment information, and recurring billing continues uninterrupted.

What if I'm still under contract with Mindbody?

We can help you navigate your transition. Many studios find that the savings from switching offset any early termination fees within the first few months.

Is there really no monthly fee?

Correct. Sweatpals has no monthly subscription fee. You only pay a small platform fee on transactions, which is typically much lower than what you're paying Mindbody.

How does the discovery marketplace work?

Your classes are automatically shown to our 1M+ active users searching for fitness experiences. We also run paid advertising campaigns promoting your studio at no cost to you.

Can I try Sweatpals before fully migrating?

Absolutely. You can start listing experiences on Sweatpals alongside your existing Mindbody setup to test the platform before making a full switch.
